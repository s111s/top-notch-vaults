How it works

  1. Pick a strategy

    Compare profit, drawdown and how many investors follow each strategy on the leaderboard. Each has an honest backtest and an independent audit.

  2. Fund your vault

    Your vault is your own NEAR account. You set the tokens, the cap per trade and the trades per day. One vault is live on mainnet today; self-serve vault creation is next.

  3. The agent trades

    Each day it reads the strategy's on-chain signal and rebalances through NEAR Intents. The researcher gets paid inside each swap.

  4. You stay in control

    Read the signed daily note. Pause, switch strategy or withdraw from your wallet at any time. The agent can't stop you.

The NEAR stack behind it

NEAR Intents · 1Click

Every trade, best price, any chain's token

The agent swaps through the 1Click API. The researcher's and platform's fees are paid inside each swap with appFees: no billing system, no custody.

NEAR smart contract

A vault only you can empty

Your vault is a NEAR account whose only full-access key is yours. Its Rust contract lets the agent call one method, swap, within your caps.

NEAR AI Cloud · TEE

A note you can verify

Each day a model running in a trusted enclave writes what your agent did and signs it. Your browser checks the signature against the live attestation.

NEAR registry

Track records that can't be backdated

Strategy cards, daily signals and audit reports are timestamped on-chain, append-only. Hidden logic is pinned by a hash.
